# Arsenal-1: One Year In

Arsenal-1: One Year In

A year ago, we announced we would build Arsenal-1 in Pickaway County, Ohio, the first defense manufacturing facility of its kind. At the time, people understandably asked whether we were serious.

One year later, here’s the answer:

Breaking ground.
Construction on Building 1 is well underway and will encompass 775,000 square feet of production space and 120,000 additional square feet of office and support space. We broke ground on Building 2 (924,000+ sq ft) last summer. Today, crews are raising the walls and will start internal construction soon.

We’ve also finalized the full site plan, locking in a 10-year buildout that will bring Arsenal-1 to full scale by 2035. This plan includes additional manufacturing and warehouse buildings, a centralized hub, a substation, facilities operations buildings, roadwork, and a campus amenities building. Rather than coming online all at once, each building will go live in a staggered cadence, allowing us to scale intentionally while staying aligned with production demands.

Building a presence in Ohio.
We're working directly with Pickaway County officials and state engineers on road and traffic design. Our leaders have connected with a slate of community partners and schools who are invested in Arsenal’s development and we look forward to connecting with many more.

Building the team.
Anduril will be a major employer in Ohio and we are excited to empower Ohioans to lead our growth here. Our initial 25 Ohio employees came onboard in July and have been training at Anduril HQ in CA. These early hires are technical production and manufacturing roles, we see them as core to our commitment to manufacture at scale at Arsenal-1. These leaders have started returning to Ohio to launch full-scale hiring and begin production in Building 1. We hired more than 50 people for Arsenal-1 in 2025 and hiring is well underway for our next Arsenal cohorts. Please check out our jobs page for the latest opportunities to come build with us.

We’re also growing our presence beyond the factory floor. We recently hired Kevin Kerr, Vice President of Production for Arsenal-1; David Frash, Director of State and Local Government Relations; and Kristen Easterday, Director of External Affairs who will be based in central Ohio—a reflection of our long-term commitment to the region.

First product: YFQ-44A.
The true measures of a factory’s success are who it hires and what it builds. We’re hiring the best of Ohio to build something big. The first product at Arsenal-1 will be YFQ-44A, a missionized variant of our Fury autonomous fighter jet for the U.S. Air Force’s Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA) program. Through its focus on autonomy and affordable mass, the CCA program is driving a paradigm shift in how the United States will employ and project combat airpower this decade and beyond.

It’s not enough to just build YFQ-44A, though. It needs to be done with all possible speed. As our adversaries advance, scaling CCA is fundamental to our national security. Arsenal-1 will be at the frontier of our rapid pace. We took YFQ-44A from clean-sheet design to fully-built aircraft in 365 days, started flight testing in October, and we’re on the hook to deliver a real, operational capability by the end of the decade. That timeline is extremely aggressive in comparison to every other fighter aircraft program in recent history.

Ohio has always been ahead of the rest in pushing the boundaries of aviation. We see no reason to halt that tradition with YFQ-44A. We will begin production of YFQ-44A in Arsenal-1's Building 1 by Q2.

A new American factory for new American technologies.
Arsenal-1 is the future of American defense manufacturing. The systems it will produce will change the way we fight. They will prevent war by enabling our military to dominate the battlespace and they will be made right here in Ohio.

YFQ-44A will be the first capability we build at Arsenal-1, but far from the last. Multiple programs will move production to Arsenal-1 over the next 12 months as we continue the buildout of the site.

What comes next.
Over the coming months, work at Arsenal-1 will remain focused on:

Advancing construction across initial facilities

Expanding hiring and training of Ohio-based employees

Standing up manufacturing systems and production operations

Anduril will continue to provide updates as Arsenal-1 progresses toward initial operations.

Arsenal-1 is a long-term investment in American manufacturing capacity and Ohio’s workforce. As work continues, Anduril remains committed to building and sustaining the industrial capabilities required for the next generation of defense systems.
